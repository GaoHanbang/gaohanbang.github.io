<p>
I delivered this graduate-level module in French for M1/M2 engineering students. The course focused on end-to-end digital engineering
workflows in 3DEXPERIENCE, from modelling to simulation and validation, with applications to robotics and equipment design.
</p>

<h2>Student audience</h2>
<p>
Master’s-level engineering students (M1/M2).
</p>

<h2>Teaching language</h2>
<p>
French.
</p>

<h2>Content overview</h2>
<ul>
  <li>3DEXPERIENCE workflow: modelling-to-simulation pipeline and data organisation</li>
  <li>Serial robot-arm modelling (including KUKA examples) for simulation</li>
  <li>Multibody modelling foundations for robotics/equipment simulation</li>
  <li>Equipment design and integration; assembly and process simulation (incl. PPR use cases)</li>
</ul>

<h2>Learning outcomes</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Build consistent digital engineering workflows suitable for industrial projects</li>
  <li>Model robot/equipment systems and prepare them for simulation and validation</li>
  <li>Apply assembly and process simulation concepts to equipment integration scenarios</li>
</ul>
